# Code of Conduct

Invest Lens is a learning project, and everyone is welcome to take part in it.

## Our standard

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and explain your reasoning.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Welcome beginners. Questions are fine.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- No spam, and no promotion of specific investments or financial products.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, open a private report through the repository's Security tab or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions.
